A stroll from the pub

A short walk to see the college animals and an old church.

  1. The college animals

    From the pub, follow the footpath towards Plumpton College, which farms the land at the foot of the Downs. Look out for sheep, llamas and birds. It is a working college, so keep to the public footpath.

  2. The old church

    It stands within the college grounds. It is a medieval church with old wall paintings inside; it may not always be open. Then wander back to the pub, where the bar stocks wine from the college’s own vineyard.

Getting there

Open in Google Maps

We might have a shuttle back from the venue to Brighton!

By train

Direct trains run from London Victoria to Lewes in just over an hour, and from Brighton the journey to Lewes takes around 15 minutes. Check times from London Victoria. Lewes is a short taxi ride from the pub. The nearest station, Plumpton, is about two miles away, but Lewes has the most trains. Buses are also available; see below.

By bus

Compass Travel buses 166 and 167 run from Lewes and stop at Plumpton College, a short walk from the pub. They are infrequent, so check the times before you rely on them. Bus times for Plumpton.

By car

Use the postcode BN7 3AF. The pub is on Ditchling Road. Parking available at the venue.

Coming from abroad to Brighton

The easiest routes are by air or by Eurostar, then by train. The Trainline app is a good way to book your UK train tickets.

By air: fly to London Gatwick, take a train to Brighton (about 30 minutes). You can also fly to Heathrow: a National Express coach (route 025) runs from Heathrow to Brighton in about two hours. Heathrow to Brighton coaches.

By Eurostar: arrive at London St Pancras International (King’s Cross is next door) and take a direct train to Brighton, which takes around an hour and a half.

Things to do

Making a weekend of it? Pick from our favourite places on the Downs and along the coast.

Things to do in Brighton

  • Brighton Beach & Palace Pier

    The classic pebble beach, with a fairground pier for an ice cream or a go on the arcades.

    Free to walk
    Visit Brighton

  • The Lanes & North Laine

    Narrow lanes packed with independent shops, cafes and vintage finds. Easy to lose an afternoon in.

    Free to browse

  • Royal Pavilion & Gardens

    The former royal palace's lavish, Indo-Saracenic rooms are worth the ticket price, with gardens to wander outside.

    Ticketed
    Royal Pavilion & Gardens

  • Stanmer Park & One Garden

    A green valley on the edge of Brighton, with woodland walks and Stanmer House. One Garden Brighton is a free walled garden run by Plumpton College.

    Free entry
    One Garden Brighton

  • Restaurants & pubs

    A few favourites: the Lion & Lobster (try the sticky toffee pudding), an old Brighton pub with a roof terrace; Bincho Yakitori, Japanese skewers on Preston Street; Palmito, a tiny Latin American spot on Western Road; No Catch, vegan fish and chips on King's Road by the seafront; Captain's Fish and Chips, a classic fish and chip restaurant on the seafront by the Palace Pier; Lucky Beach, a relaxed beachfront café and bar on the King's Road Arches; Moksha Caffe, a cosy brunch spot near York Place; Oeuf, an all-day brunch café in Hove, steps from the seafront; the Garden Café in St Ann's Well Gardens in Hove; and Fiddler's Elbow, a lively Irish pub in the Lanes with live music.

Day trips from Brighton

  • Ditchling Beacon

    The highest point in East Sussex, 248 metres up, with views across the Weald and out to the Channel. Walk along the ridge or head down to Stanmer.

    Breeze bus 79
    Mostly weekends and bank holidays
    Bus info

  • Devil’s Dyke

    A huge dry valley cut into the Downs above Brighton, with views for miles. Walk down for a pub lunch at the Shepherd and Dog in Fulking (about a mile) or the Royal Oak in Poynings.

    Breeze bus 77, about 35 min
    Timetable

  • Lewes

    Historic county town with a hilltop castle, steep cobbled streets, independent shops and Harvey’s Brewery.

    Train from Brighton,
    about 15 min

  • Arundel

    Castle, cathedral and riverside. AllTrails has an easy loop from the town past the castle, Swanbourne Lake and the wetland centre.

    Train from Brighton,
    one change, about an hour
    AllTrails loop

  • Seven Sisters

    The chalk cliffs along the coast near Seaford, with a classic walk from Cuckmere Haven. Good for a windswept afternoon.

    Coaster bus 12/12A/13X
    Timetable
